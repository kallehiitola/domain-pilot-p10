Method and assumptions

Resin mold volume calculator equation

Rectangle volume = L × W × depth; Cylinder volume = π × (diameter / 2)² × depth; Mixed batch = (mold volume − displacement) × mold count × (1 + extra / 100).

Step-by-step logic

Compute cavity volume in cubic centimeters (1 cm³ = 1 mL; 1 US fl oz = 29.5735 mL). Subtract the volume displaced by embedded wood or inclusions, multiply by the number of molds, and apply your mixing-cup allowance. When your resin specifies a mixing ratio by volume (A:B), divide the total in those exact proportions.

Units and rounding

Linear dimensions convert at 2.54 cm per inch and liquid volumes convert at exactly 29.5735296 mL per US fluid ounce. Small batches automatically retain extra decimal precision so A:B ratios stay accurate.

Limits and edge cases

Only use the A:B component split when the manufacturer specifies a ratio by volume—never apply a volume split to a resin mixed by weight, because resin and hardener densities differ. Respect each resin maximum single-pour thickness to avoid overheating, bubbling, or cracking.
