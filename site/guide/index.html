How to how much resin do i need

Calculate rectangular or cylindrical mold volume, subtract displaced volume and add a chosen allowance. Optional component volumes apply only when the manufacturer specifies a ratio by volume.

Quick answer: A 10 × 5 × 2 cm rectangular mold with no displacement and 10% extra needs 110 mL of mixed product.

What you need to measure

  • Rectangle length
  • Rectangle width
  • Cylinder diameter
  • Fill depth
  • Number of molds
  • Displaced volume per mold
  • Extra allowance
  • Product ratio: parts A by volume
  • Product ratio: parts B by volume
  • Mold shape: choose the option that matches your layout or product instructions.
  • My product specifies a mixing ratio by volume: choose the option that matches your layout or product instructions.

How to calculate it

Rectangular volume is length × width × depth; cylindrical volume is π × (diameter ÷ 2)² × depth. A cubic centimetre is one millilitre. Subtract displaced volume per mold, multiply by mold count, then add the allowance. For a stated volume ratio A:B, split the total in those proportions.

What changes the estimate

The example 2:1 ratio is illustrative. Enter the exact ratio and measurement basis from your product instructions. Do not use the component-volume results for a ratio specified by mass. Do not guess density to convert a mass ratio. Follow the manufacturer’s mixing, batch-size and handling instructions.

Worked example

A 10 × 5 × 2 cm rectangular mold with no displacement and 10% extra needs 110 mL of mixed product.
